# XeLaTeX page labels: one pass, two passes, and one pass over an edited deck (docs/scientific/12 §5.1)
# measured 2026-10-17 on: XeTeX 3.141592653-2.6-0.999998 and LuaHBTeX 1.24.0 (TeX Live 2026/Arch Linux),
# beamer 2026/01/22 v3.77, hyperref 2026-01-29 v7.01p (driver hxetex.def for xelatex); labels read with pdf.js
# (pdfjs-dist getPageLabels), ranges with qpdf 12.4.2 (--json=2 --json-key=pagelabels).
#
# The registration below was written to a file before the first compile; it is reproduced verbatim.

  H-XSTALE (registered 2026-10-17, before any run)
  
  Claim from hxetex.def (hyperref's xdvipdfmx driver): at \begin{document} it writes /PageLabels from the
  \HyPL@Entry lines the PREVIOUS run left in the .aux, and only warns "Rerun to get /PageLabels entry" when
  there are none. So a single xelatex pass over an EDITED deck carries the previous version's labels.
  
  Design: v1 = a title frame + 5 frames x 3 overlays (16 pages). v2 = v1 with one 2-overlay frame inserted
  after the title (18 pages).
    step 1: xelatex v1 (no .aux)      -> expected labels: none (null), log warns "Rerun to get /PageLabels entry"
    step 2: xelatex v1 again          -> expected: 1 2 2 2 3 3 3 4 4 4 5 5 5 6 6 6
    step 3: v2 replaces the source; xelatex ONCE (v1's .aux present)
                                     -> expected: v1's labels for pages 0..15, then the last range continued:
                                        1 2 2 2 3 3 3 4 4 4 5 5 5 6 6 6 7 8   (stale; no Rerun warning)
    step 4: xelatex v2 again          -> expected true labels: 1 2 2 3 3 3 4 4 4 5 5 5 6 6 6 7 7 7
  Control: the same four steps with lualatex -> expected true labels at every step (v1 at steps 1-2, v2 at 3-4).
  Refutation: step 3 under xelatex gives v2's true labels, or no labels.

## Verdict

  Steps 1, 2 and 4 under xelatex and all four control steps under lualatex: as registered.
  Step 3 under xelatex: the labels are v1's, with no Rerun warning, as registered: the staleness is confirmed.
  The registered labels of the two pages past v1's end (7 8) were wrong: they read 6 6. hyperref writes each
  Beamer frame number as a range with a fixed prefix (/P (6), no numbering style /S), one range per frame, so
  every later page repeats the last prefix. The registration had assumed decimal-numbered ranges.

## The stale pass is silent

  Warnings in the xelatex logs: step 1 has 'Package hyperref Warning: Rerun to get /PageLabels entry.' and
  'Package rerunfilecheck Warning: File `deck.out' has changed.'; step 3 (the stale pass) has no warning at all.
