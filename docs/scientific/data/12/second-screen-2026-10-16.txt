## Findings
1. Geometry: every page doubles the paper — 4:3 362.835 x 272.126 becomes 725.669 x 272.126 (right / left) and
   362.835 x 544.252 (top / bottom). The side names where the NOTES go; the slide is the other half
   (right: slide at x 8.5–96 and 28–92 of 725.7, i.e. the left half; top: slide at y 276–404 of 544.3, the bottom half).
2. The notes half repeats the slide's own words: the default note template draws a thumbnail of the slide
   (FrameOneTitle at x 637, SlideOneBody at x 642 in notes-right) — a search over whole pages finds them twice.
3. Page labels: show notes on second screen CORRUPTS them, undetectably. big-plain is labelled 1 1 2 3 3 3 4 5 5
   (true frames); big-notes, the same source with notes, 1 1 1 2 2 3 3 4 3. single-notes (true 1 2 3 4 5 5) is
   labelled 1 1 2 2 3 3 and nonotes-split (true 1 1 2 3 3) 1 1 1 2 2 — WELL-FORMED sequences (start at 1, steps of
   0 or +1) that are wrong, so no consistency check can tell corrupt labels from real ones.
4. previous slide on second screen (big-prev) doubles the page the same way but keeps correct labels; geometry
   cannot tell it from a notes deck.
5. Plain 'show notes' (big-shownotes) interleaves note pages at the slide size: slide pages carry the frame
   number, note pages their physical page number (out of scope here: no geometric signal; see the deferred
   presenter-console feature request).

## Decisions taken from these data (vinary.slides.deck)
- a uniform page size that doubles one Beamer paper in width or height is a second-screen page (finding 1);
- the slides layout shows the half opposite the notes, per the :slides-notes preference; auto = Beamer's default,
  notes right (and notes below for a vertical page);
- the other half's text is hidden from find in the slides layout (finding 2);
- a second-screen deck gets one frame per page and no labels (findings 3 and 4).
