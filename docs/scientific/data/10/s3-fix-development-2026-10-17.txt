## Hypothesis H-R1 — registered after DIAG-1, BEFORE the change (verbatim from the session ledger)
The residual tree-filter T3 lag is the renderer recomputing EVERY directory scope of every project
(tree-state/directory-scopes): twice per tree payload in events/prune-tree-ui and again on every Files render
(tree-state/effective-expanded), constructing a [root dir] vector + str/join for every (file, depth) pair.
Prediction: an index-scanning relative-directory set, memoized on project (and projects-vector) identity,
cuts directory-scope cost > 5x (micro-benchmark on this repository's listing) and T3 passes in 3/3 DIAG runs with
P1 still holding. Refuted if T3 still fails with directory-scopes no longer in the longest stretches.
